Sulfuric acid requires goggles, gloves, and a lab coat during handling if it is higher than 0.5 Molar. 
Sulfuric acid can cause severe burns to skin and eyes.

Hydrochloric acid should be stored in an acid cabinet.
Hydrochloric acid may release irritating fumes.

Ethanol is highly flammable and must be kept away from ignition sources.

Copper sulfate may irritate the skin and eyes.
Copper sulfate should not be ingested.

Sodium hydroxide requires eye protection and gloves.
Sodium hydroxide can cause severe skin burns.

Bunsen burners should be kept on yellow flame when not in use. We use blue flame when using so it does not create too much carbon dioxide in air. 

Broken glass should be disposed of in a designated sharps container.

Eye wash stations should be used immediately after chemical splashes to the eyes.

Molarity equation: M = n / V
M = Molarity
n = moles

To calculate moles:
n = M × V

Volume should be converted into liters before calculations.

Mass calculation:
mass = moles × molar mass

Sodium chloride (NaCl) molar mass = 58.44 g/mol

Copper sulfate pentahydrate molar mass = 249.68 g/mol
